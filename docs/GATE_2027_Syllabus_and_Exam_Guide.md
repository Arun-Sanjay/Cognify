## 2.3 Question types & negative marking

| Type | Options | Correct answers | Marks | Negative marking | Partial marking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MCQ** (Multiple Choice) | 4 | Exactly 1 | 1 or 2 | **−1/3** (1-mark), **−2/3** (2-mark) | No |
| **MSQ** (Multiple Select) | 4 | 1 or more | 1 or 2 | **None** | **None**: you must select exactly the right combination |
| **NAT** (Numerical Answer) | none | A signed real number, typed on a virtual keypad (the keyboard is disabled) | 1 or 2 | **None** | **None** |

- NAT answers can be integers (e.g. 10 or −10) or decimals (e.g. 10.1, 10.01, −10.001). The question states how many decimal places are required. Round off only at the end of a calculation.
- Questions can be standalone statements or data-based, combinations of options, or "match the items".
- Official examples:
  - **MCQ:** "Which Institute is organizing GATE 2027?" → (C) IIT Madras.
  - **MSQ:** "Which is/are the prime factor(s) of 96? (A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 6 (D) 15" → only the combination A + B scores.
  - **NAT:** "If the wire diameter of a compressive helical spring is increased by 2%, the change in spring stiffness (in %) is ___ (rounded off to two decimal places)."

## 2.7 Exam-day rules & timing

- You can take your seat **40 minutes** before the start. You can log in and read the instructions **20 minutes** before the start.
- **You cannot log in more than 15 minutes after the scheduled start.** You cannot leave before the scheduled end.
- The exam ends automatically when time runs out.
- Bring a **printed admit card** (A4, preferably in colour) and the **original photo ID** you gave in the application. Admit cards are not posted or emailed.
- Accepted photo IDs: Aadhaar-UID, Aadhaar Virtual ID, Passport, PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence, or any valid Central/State Government photo ID.
- Only the **on-screen virtual scientific calculator** is allowed.
- Rough work goes on **scribble pads**:
  - Write your name and registration number on the pad first.
  - You may hold only one pad at a time; return it to get the next.
  - Don't tear out sheets, and return all pads at the end.
- Some papers run in several sessions, but each candidate sits only the one session on their admit card.

## 2.8 Scoring: normalization, GATE score, qualifying marks

**Step 1: Raw marks.** Papers held in a single session use raw marks directly.

**Step 2: Normalization for papers held in several sessions.** This assumes candidates' abilities are distributed the same way across sessions.

$$\hat{M}_{ij} = \frac{\bar{M}^{g}_{t} - M^{g}_{q}}{\bar{M}_{ti} - M_{iq}}\,(M_{ij} - M_{iq}) + M^{g}_{q}$$

- $M_{ij}$ = actual marks of candidate *j* in session *i*
- $\bar{M}^{g}_{t}$ = average marks of the top 0.1% of candidates across all sessions
- $M^{g}_{q}$ = mean + standard deviation of marks across all sessions
- $\bar{M}_{ti}$ = average marks of the top 0.1% in session *i*
- $M_{iq}$ = mean + standard deviation of marks in session *i*

**Step 3: GATE score, on a scale of 1000.**

$$\text{GATE Score} = S_q + (S_t - S_q)\,\frac{M - M_q}{\bar{M}_t - M_q}$$

- $M$ = the candidate's marks (actual marks, or normalized marks for papers held in several sessions)
- $M_q$ = qualifying marks for the General category in that paper
- $\bar{M}_t$ = mean marks of the top 0.1% or top 10 candidates (whichever is larger), across all sessions
- $S_q = 350$ (the score assigned to $M_q$) and $S_t = 900$ (the score assigned to $\bar{M}_t$)

**Step 4: Qualifying marks (out of 100).**

| Category | Qualifying marks |
|---|---|
| General | **max(25, min(40, μ + σ))**, where μ and σ are the mean and standard deviation of all candidates in the paper |
| OBC-NCL / EWS | 0.9 × General |
| SC / ST / PwD | (2/3) × General |

## 2.9 Results, scorecard & validity

- After the exam, the order of events is:
  1. Candidates' responses are published in GOAPS.
  2. Provisional answer keys are published.
  3. Candidates can contest the keys for a fee.
  4. Final results and scores are announced.
  5. Qualified candidates download their scorecards.
- Results come out on **19 Mar 2027**. Scorecards can be downloaded by **qualified candidates only**, and there are no hard copies.
- After the free download window, a scorecard costs **₹500 per paper**. No scorecards are issued from **1 Jan 2028**.
- The score is **valid for 3 years**. After that, no information about the score will be available.
- Malpractice can lead to a ban from GATE for the next 3 years.

## 2.10 Eligibility

## 2.12 Application process

- **Indian nationals register through DigiLocker.** A verified (e-KYC) account is recommended.

- GOAPS is also used for:

  - downloading the admit card
  - viewing responses, marks and score

## 2.15 What a GATE score is used for

- **Postgraduate admission with financial assistance.** A GATE score gets you into Master's and Direct-PhD programs in Engineering, Technology and Architecture, and PhD programs in Engineering, Technology, Architecture, Science and Humanities. These are at MoE-supported institutes such as the IITs, IISc, NITs and CFTIs.
  - MoE guidelines say **at least 70% weight** goes to GATE performance. The rest can come from a test, an interview and/or academic record.
  - Qualifying GATE is also the minimum requirement for many government fellowships.
- **PSU recruitment.** PSUs that have used GATE scores recently: AAI, BHEL, BSNL, CIL, CRIS, CVPPL, DVC, ECIL, EIL, GAIL, GRID-India, IOCL, MDSL, NALCO, NCRTC, NHAI, NMDC, NLCIL, HVPNL, NEEPCO, NPCIL, NTPC, OPTCL, ONGC, POWERGRID, POSOCO, PSPCL, RINL, SAIL, among others.
- **Central government Group-A posts.** For example, Senior Field Officer (Tele), Senior Research Officer (Crypto) and Senior Research Officer (S&T) in the Cabinet Secretariat.
- **Disclaimer:** qualifying GATE does **not** guarantee admission, a scholarship or a job.

### Notes on conflicting figures found during research

An initial (non-official) aggregator-style web result returned different cutoff figures for a few popular papers — e.g., EC General 25.0 / OBC‑NCL‑EWS 22.5 / SC‑ST‑PwD 16.6, ME General 35.8 / 32.2 / 23.8, EE General 25.0 / 22.5 / 16.6, CE General 29.2 / 26.2 / 19.4 — which **do not match** the official IIT Guwahati PDF/cutoff page (EC 26.4/23.7/17.5; ME 25.2/22.6/16.7; EE 27.7/24.9/18.4; CE 28.7/25.8/19.1). Given the official report's internal consistency (its summed totals match independently reported national totals exactly, to the digit), the official figures in the main table above are treated as authoritative, and the conflicting aggregator figures are flagged here rather than used.
